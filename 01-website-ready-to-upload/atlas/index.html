Skåne

4 stories · 14 photographs
  • Landskrona SlottLandskrona, Skåne, Sweden · Spring 2026Christian III began the citadel at Landskrona in 1549 to hold the Öresund. It is the best-preserved sixteenth-century castle in the Nordic countries — largely because it spent two hundred years as a prison instead of a ruin.
  • Ales StenarAles Stenar, Kåseberga, Skåne, Sweden · Spring 2026Fifty-nine boulders in the outline of a ship, sixty-seven metres long, on a headland above the Baltic at Kåseberga. What Ales Stenar was for is still argued over — grave, cult site, or calendar.
  • The Stronghold at GlimmingehusGlimmingehus, Hammenhög, Sweden · Spring 2026Standing alone among the fields of southeastern Skåne, Glimmingehus is the best-preserved medieval stronghold in Scandinavia — a fortified house raised between 1499 and 1506, when this corner of Sweden still answered to the Danish crown.
  • Kärnan: Helsingborg's Medieval KeepKärnan, Helsingborg, Sweden · Spring 2026Helsingborg's brick keep, Kärnan, has watched over the Sound for some seven centuries — all that remains of a great medieval castle, reached today by a monumental terrace staircase climbing straight up from the old town.
